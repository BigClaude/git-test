Classic Garlic Butter Pasta

⏱️ Prep Time: 5 mins | 🔥 Cook Time: 10 mins | 🍽️ Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200g Spaghetti or any pasta of choice
  • 3 tablespoons Unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves Garlic, finely minced
  • 1/4 cup Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley (optional, chopped for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to the package directions until al dente.
  2. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain the remaining pasta and set it aside.
  3. In a large skillet over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant (be careful not to burn it).
  4. Add the cooked pasta and 2–3 tablespoons of the reserved pasta water into the skillet with the garlic butter. Toss continuously to coat the pasta.
  5. Remove from heat, stir in the grated Parmesan cheese, and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired, and serve warm!
